🎯 TRADING STRATEGY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STRATEGY: MA + Support/Resistance + Bollinger Bands

BUY SIGNAL (Confluence):
───────────────────────
✓ Price > MA20 > MA50       (Uptrend confirmed)
✓ Price > Support Level     (Not below support)
✓ Price < Upper BB          (Not overbought)
✓ Candlestick Bullish       (Green close)

When ALL 4 conditions met → BUY SIGNAL

SELL SIGNAL (Confluence):
────────────────────────
✓ Price < MA20 < MA50       (Downtrend confirmed)
✓ Price < Resistance Level  (Not above resistance)
✓ Price > Lower BB          (Not oversold)
✓ Candlestick Bearish       (Red close)

When ALL 4 conditions met → SELL SIGNAL

HOLD SIGNAL:
───────────
- When conditions don't align
- No confluence
- Waiting for next opportunity
